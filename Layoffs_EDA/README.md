Project: Layoffs Dataset - Exploratory Data Analysis

Objective:
Explore trends and patterns in global layoffs after data cleaning.

Analysis Includes:
- Maximum layoffs and layoff percentages
- Companies with 100% layoffs
- Total layoffs by company, industry, country, and stage
- Year-wise layoffs trend
- Rolling monthly total of layoffs
- Top 5 companies with highest layoffs each year

Tools:
- MySQL

Outcome:
Identified high-impact companies, industries, countries, and time periods affected by layoffs.
